Mycopunk Co-op Guide — Squad Roles & Survival Tips

Mycopunk is built for 2–4 players, but most squads wipe because everyone plays solo. Fix your roles, your comms and your extraction timing, and hard zones turn into farm runs.

Split roles before you drop

Mixed squads clear roughly twice as fast as four players running the same DPS build. Agree on these roles in the lobby — it takes ten seconds and saves the run:

  • The Fighter — highest-DPS build (Double-Barrel + Shield Drone). Takes the front, breaks elites first.
  • The Looter — Spore SMG + Toxic Dash. Fastest movement, opens every cache and spore cache on the route.
  • The Support — revive kit mandatory. Carries extra medkits, revives downed teammates, watches the flanks.
  • The Fourth (flex) — crowd control (Flamethrower + Thorns) for horde zones, or a second DPS for boss contracts.

The non-negotiable: one revive kit per squad

On hard zones, a downed teammate is not a death — it is a 15-second window. If nobody can revive fast, one mistake snowballs into a full wipe and everyone loses their backpack. At least one player runs a revive-focused kit every single run, no exceptions. The Marksman + Cloak build makes a safe reviver because you can reach downed teammates without trading hits.

Comms habits that prevent wipes

  1. Ping everything. Loot, elites, caches, exit timers. Five seconds of pinging saves a whole run.
  2. Call the storm. Whoever sees the spore storm warning calls extraction immediately. Nobody argues.
  3. Revive order is agreed, not guessed. Closest player revives; everyone else holds angles.
  4. Share rare drops. Pass gear your build can't use to the player who can — the squad's power is the sum.

Public lobby etiquette (how to get carried)

Random squads will happily carry a geared-but-quiet player. They will not carry a loot-hog. Stay with the group, ping what you find, don't hoard rare drops your build can't use, and never extract alone with the squad's loot — that's how you get friend requests instead of kicks.

Squad farming tip: split into two pairs on Zone 2 loops — one pair pulls the horde pit, the other sweeps the edge caches, then swap. Loot per hour goes up roughly 40% versus staying together.

Boss fights: the 30-second brief

Before every boss contract, agree on three things: who taunts, who focuses adds, and where the safe corner is. Most boss wipes are not damage problems — they are four players panicking in four directions.
