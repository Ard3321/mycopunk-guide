Mycopunk Beginner Guide — Your First 5 Hours, Done Right

The opening hours of Mycopunk decide how smoothly the rest of the game goes. This walkthrough covers the right order to do things, the one weapon to upgrade first, and the three mistakes that leave new players broke at level 10.

Step 1 — Clear the tutorial contracts before anything else

The first three contracts are not just a tutorial — they are your starter bankroll. They teach dash-timing, loot pickup and extraction under zero risk, and they pay enough to fully upgrade your first weapon. Players who skip them to chase the open map end up under-geared and frustrated. Budget about 45 minutes here.

Step 2 — Pick ONE weapon and pour everything into it

Mycopunk's upgrade economy punishes variety. Upgrading five guns a little leaves you weak everywhere; upgrading one gun a lot carries you through the entire mid-game. For most players the answer is the Spore SMG: it is cheap to upgrade, forgiving at range, and its on-hit heal keeps you alive while you learn enemy patterns.

Rule of thumb: don't touch a second weapon until your primary is at +5 or you find a blue replacement. Everything else is scrap currency — see the loot farming guide.

Step 3 — Run the first zone on normal until it feels boring

Difficulty in Mycopunk is a gear check disguised as a skill check. Moving to hard zones early means faster deaths, lost loot, and wasted contract timers. Stay on normal in Zone 1 until you can clear a contract without dropping below half health. That usually takes 3–4 runs. Then — and only then — step up.

Step 4 — Learn the grey/green/blue economy

  • Grey (junk): sell on sight. It is your primary currency for upgrades and ammo.
  • Green (common gear): keep as a bridge until a blue upgrade drops. Don't invest materials in it.
  • Blue (rare): the real power spikes. Prioritize weapon > dash module > armor when spending rare materials.

Step 5 — Join public co-op at level 10+

Public lobbies are the fastest way to learn the maps, and carried contracts pay better than solo grinding. You do not need to be good — you need to ping loot and stay near the squad. The co-op guide covers roles and etiquette so you're a welcome teammate, not a liability.

The three mistakes that waste your first 5 hours

  1. Buying cosmetics early. Looks don't clear contracts. Every credit goes into gear until you hit blue rarity.
  2. Chasing mission markers. The best loot is off the path, in spore caches along zone edges — not at the objective.
  3. Extracting late. A full backpack lost to a spore storm is worth zero. Extract with 30 seconds to spare, every run.

What "good" looks like after 5 hours

By now you should have: one fully upgraded primary weapon, at least two blue drops, a cleared path through Zone 1 on normal, and a friends list from public lobbies. If that's where you are, you're ahead of the curve — the weapons tier list is your next stop.
