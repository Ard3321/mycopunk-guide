Privacy Policy

The short version: we count visits to improve the guides, we never sell your data, and you can ask us to delete anything we hold.

1. Who we are

Mycopunk Guide Hub ("we", "the site") is a fan-made guide website available at mycopunk-guide. For any privacy question or request, reach us through the contact page.

2. What data we collect

Analytics data. We use privacy-friendly analytics services (currently Microsoft Clarity, with Google Analytics and Bing Webmaster Tools data where enabled) to understand which guides are read, where visitors come from, and how pages are used. These services may collect: pages visited, time on page, referring site, approximate country/region, device type, browser, and screen size. This data is aggregated and is not used to identify you personally.

Email addresses. Only if you voluntarily subscribe to the weekly digest. Your email is used solely to send that newsletter and is never sold or shared. Every email includes an unsubscribe link.

What we never collect: we do not require an account to read the site, and we do not ask for or store names, addresses, payment details, or game account credentials.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

Analytics providers may set cookies or use local storage to distinguish new versus returning visits and to produce heatmaps and session recordings. Microsoft Clarity's data handling is described in the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Where Google Analytics is enabled, Google's cookie use is described in the Google advertising and cookies policy. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time; the guides remain fully readable without them.

4. Advertising

This site may display ads served by third-party networks such as Google AdSense (where enabled in your region). These vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings or via your browser's cookie controls. We do not pass any personal data to advertisers beyond what these services collect directly.

5. External links

Our guides link to official patch notes, store pages, and community resources. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of external sites.

6. Children's privacy

The site is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data (for example via the newsletter), contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

Depending on your region (including the EEA, UK, and California), you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold about you — in practice, this means your newsletter email address if you subscribed. Send requests through the contact page and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the "last updated" date at the top of the page will change with it. Continuing to use the site after an update means you accept the revised policy.
