THE GAMEWhat Is Mycopunk?

A 60-second orientation before you dive into the guides.

Mycopunk is a co-op extraction shooter set in a fungal-overgrown ruin of a collapsed world. You play a scavenger running contracts in a contaminated zone: fight through mutated flora and fauna, loot what you can carry, extract before the spore storms roll in, and upgrade your kit between runs.

Three things make it click: the dash-heavy movement (every build lives or dies by mobility), the rarity economy (grey junk is currency, blue gear is power), and the contract loop (short, repeatable missions that always pay better than long grinds). Master those three and the rest of the game opens up fast.

This is a fan-made guide hub, not affiliated with the developer. All info is verified against the current October 2026 patch.

FAQQuick Answers

The questions new players ask most.

Is Mycopunk free to play?

It is a paid title, currently discounted at launch. Check the store page for the latest price.

Can I play Mycopunk solo?

Yes. The whole campaign is soloable, though co-op makes contracts and boss fights noticeably faster. See the co-op guide for squad setups.

What's the max level?

The current cap is 50, raised with each major content update. Focus on gear score, not just level.

Does loot scale with difficulty?

Yes. Higher difficulties drop higher-rarity gear, but only after you can reliably clear the zone. The loot farming guide covers when to move up.

Is there cross-play?

Cross-play between PC platforms is supported at launch. Check the official patch notes for console plans.

Which weapon should I start with?

The Spore SMG — cheap to upgrade, forgiving, and strong into the mid-game. Full reasoning in the weapons tier list.
