About Mycopunk Guide Hub

A fan-made guide site built by players, for players — kept honest and kept updated.

Who we are

Mycopunk Guide Hub is a solo fan project run by a small group of Mycopunk players. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the developer or publisher of Mycopunk. We just play the game, take notes, and write down what actually works.

What we cover

  • Beginner guides — the first-hours walkthrough, mistakes to avoid, and how to beat the early-game wall.
  • Weapon tier lists — every ranked build with the reasoning, re-checked after each patch.
  • Loot and farming routes — loop routes, cache locations, and the drop economy explained.
  • Co-op and boss strategies — squad roles, revive kits, and hard-zone etiquette.

How our guides are verified

Every guide on this site is tested in-game before it is published: routes are run multiple times, tier rankings are based on actual clear times, and numbers are cross-checked against official patch notes. When a patch changes the meta, we update the affected pages and note the change date at the top of each guide.

How we stay updated

Mycopunk gets content updates regularly, and the meta shifts with every one. We re-test the major guides after each patch and send a short weekly digest covering what changed. Our goal is simple: if a page on this site says something, you can trust it for the current patch.

How the site stays free

Running a guide site costs time and server money, so this site is supported by advertising and affiliate links where applicable. Ads never influence our rankings or recommendations — a build is S-tier because it clears contracts fastest, not because it pays more. We do not sell or share reader data; see our privacy policy for details.

Spotted an error or something outdated? We genuinely want to hear about it — see the contact page.
