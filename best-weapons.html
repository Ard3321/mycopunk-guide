Why the Spore SMG is still number one

The Spore SMG wins on consistency, not peak damage. Its on-hit heal means mistakes cost you health tempo instead of a full medkit, and its ammo economy lets you clear a whole contract on two resupplies. After the October patch raised its base mag size by 4, there is no situation where another weapon clears a standard contract faster.

Pair it with the Toxic Dash module: dash damage applies the spore dot, which both chips elites and triggers the heal. That single combo is the backbone of the current meta.

The best build for each situation

  • Solo farming runs: Spore SMG + Toxic Dash. Safe, fast, cheap to maintain.
  • Co-op boss fights: Double-Barrel + Shield Drone. Stand next to the boss, out-damage its heal.
  • Learning a hard zone: Marksman Rifle + Cloak Module. Die less, learn patterns, loot what others leave.
  • Swarm events: Flamethrower + Thorns Armor. Let the horde come to you.

How to read tiers as a beginner

Tier lists assume full upgrades. A fully-upgraded B-tier gun beats a stock S-tier one. If you are still on your first weapon, ignore this page's rankings and follow the beginner guide instead — it tells you exactly which gun to max first and what to do with everything else.
