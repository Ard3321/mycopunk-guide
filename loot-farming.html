Mycopunk Loot Farming Guide — Best Routes & Drop Tips

Loot per hour in Mycopunk is a routing problem, not a luck problem. These are the routes, cache spots and habits that geared players run — and the ones that quietly double your materials.

The golden rule: run the loop, not the mission

The fastest loot in Mycopunk comes from repeating one short zone with every container opened — not from chasing mission markers across the map. A clean 4-minute loop with 6–8 containers beats a 12-minute "do everything" run every single time. Pick a Zone 1 or Zone 2 loop you can run from memory, and repeat it.

Route 1 — Zone 1 "Edge Sweep" (best for beginners)

  • Drop at the north extraction point and sweep the map edge counter-clockwise.
  • Open every spore cache — the glowing mushrooms hugging the zone border hide the best blue drops.
  • Ignore the central compound until your last two minutes; it is the hottest part of the map.
  • Extract from the south point. Full loop: about 4 minutes.

Route 2 — Zone 2 "Horde Pit" (best for materials)

Once you can clear Zone 1 on hard, the Zone 2 horde pit pays the best grey-junk-per-minute in the game. Bring a swarm-control build (Flamethrower + Thorns is ideal) and farm the two-minute spawn waves, extracting between waves. You will drown in upgrade currency — which is exactly what your S-tier build needs.

Spore caches: the blue-drop lottery

Spore caches respawn every 20–30 minutes of play time and always drop at least one green, with a strong blue chance on higher difficulties. They are deliberately placed off the critical path — along cliffs, under bridges, behind waterfalls. If your route doesn't include at least three cache detours, it isn't a farming route, it's a speedrun.

Farming during limited-time events is the single biggest multiplier in the game. Event periods boost drop rates across the board — check the event board before every session and plan your loops around it.

What to keep, what to sell

  • Sell all grey immediately. Bank space is the real limit on loot per hour.
  • Keep greens only as bridges until a blue replacement for that slot drops.
  • Rare materials > rare gear. A blue weapon you can't upgrade is worth less than the materials to max the one you have.
  • Never extract late. Loot in your backpack when the storm hits is loot you never had.

When to move up a difficulty

Move up when you can clear your current loop twice in a row without dying. Higher difficulties raise drop rarity, but a death costs you the entire backpack — the math only works when your clear rate is reliable.
